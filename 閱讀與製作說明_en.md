# Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva: Collected Teachings — About This Site

This site gathers 56 records provided by the author: dream teachings, dialogues of inquiry with Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva, plain-language renderings, and accounts from fellow practitioners. They range from questions of practice to family, loss and everyday life.

## The texts and their translation

- The Chinese texts are kept exactly as received. Only layout, topics, short introductions and section headings were added by the site.
- The English translation was prepared separately for this site. The Bodhisattva's teachings are rendered in a formal, scriptural register; the plain renderings in everyday English. **Where the two differ, the Chinese original is authoritative.**
- The translation has been submitted for further review by a second AI system; revisions will be made as that review is completed.
- These records are presented as the author's personal faith and experience. They are not canonical scripture, nor verified words of the Bodhisattva. Quotations of famous people and scriptures within them have not all been checked against outside sources.
- Reading notes are added only to articles touching on mental-health crises, medical decisions and personal health. They do not change the author's words.

## Features

- Search titles and full text; filter by seven topics, with article counts.
- Read the full text, the teaching alone, or the plain rendering alone; adjust text size; save articles on this device.
- Returning to the collection keeps your search, topic and position; each article remembers where you stopped reading; use ← → on a keyboard to move between articles.
- Background music plays chants from YouTube through YouTube's official player; it can be paused or changed at the lower left. Browsers may start it muted until you tap the page.
- Works on phones, tablets and desktops; supports keyboard focus and reduced-motion settings.
