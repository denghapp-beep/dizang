🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva: people often say, "First fate, second fortune, third feng shui, fourth accumulating hidden virtue, fifth study." Could the Bodhisattva share your view?

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

Fate is the frame of the boat,
fortune is the flowing water.
Feng shui lends momentum,
virtue can change the course;
study brings understanding of principle,
wisdom can discern how to act.
Yet beings are often deluded,
clinging to fate and forgetting the heart,
seeking outside and losing the root.

Fate is not cast in iron,
fortune is not forever fixed;
when a good thought arises,
it turns subtle causes;
when good deeds accumulate long,
they shift the great current.
If you only ask about fate
and do not cultivate yourself;
if you only seek fortune
and do not set your heart right,
it is like wanting to farm a field
without sowing seed.

So fate may be observed,
fortune may be examined,
feng shui may help,
virtue may be deepened,
books may bring clarity;
yet gathering all these methods together,
it still comes down to one heart.
