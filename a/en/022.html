On the Merits of the Infinite Life Sutra Compared With the Diamond Sutra and the Ksitigarbha Sutra

A record of the author's question comparing the Infinite Life Sutra, the Diamond Sutra and the Ksitigarbha Sutra.

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva about the merits and benefits of the Infinite Life Sutra compared with the Diamond Sutra and the Ksitigarbha Sutra.

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

Good man, the Infinite Life Sutra that you ask about is indeed a sutra of great causes and conditions for rebirth and Buddhahood. It emphasizes vowing to be reborn in the Pure Land, and so speaks much of future liberation and adorned Buddha-lands. The good roots planted by this sutra are deep beyond compare, bearing directly the great pure fruit of lives to come.

Yet its merit is not only for future lives; in this life too one is protected and kept in mind. Why?

First, reciting it purifies the mind, removing evil and increasing good, so that adversity naturally lessens in this life.

Second, Amitabha Buddha's original vow embraces the practitioner; the heart is at peace and blessings gradually increase. Though seeking worldly blessings is not its aim, blessings still come along with faith and vows.

Third, the sutra often says that humans and devas shall receive excellent blessings; so there is protection in this life as well.

If the three sutras are compared:

• The Ksitigarbha Sutra emphasizes karmic results in this life, benefit to both the living and the departed, and the deliverance of family members.

• The Diamond Sutra emphasizes liberation through wisdom, breaking attachment to realize emptiness, and purity of mind in this life.

• The Infinite Life Sutra emphasizes the vow to go to the Western Land, liberation in the next life, and ultimate peace and joy.

Yet all three are the Buddha's skillful means; their emphases differ, but their merits are not higher or lower — each fits the capacities of different beings.
