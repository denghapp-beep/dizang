No.58

Love Without Control, Give Without Demanding — The Bodhisattva's Reminder to Those Eager to Find a Partner

Before looking for a partner, first cultivate your own mind: non-attachment, steady emotions, responsibility, compassion and mindful observation, so that you learn to love without controlling.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

🔶 I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva:

A request on love and relationships: before anything else, I should cultivate my own mind. What should I cultivate, and how?

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

Good man, you ask how to cultivate your own mind first. This question matters more than hurrying to look for the right relationship. To cultivate the mind is not to become another person, but to see your own habitual tendencies clearly and gradually tame body, speech and mind. You may begin with five things: First, cultivate non-attachment. When you meet someone you like, do not rush to decide that she is where you belong; and when the other person responds coldly, do not keep guessing, torn between gaining and losing.

To like someone, you may do so sincerely; but you may not, because you like her, demand that she respond in the same way. Second, cultivate your emotions. When things go against your wishes, do not rush to reply. When afflictions arise in the mind, first watch your own thoughts, and ask yourself: am I expressing sincerity, or demanding that the other person meet my expectations? Third, cultivate responsibility. Your life, work, finances and commitments should be put in order step by step.

Do not expect that when another person appears, that person will settle for you every unease in your life. Fourth, cultivate compassion. Care for others without seeking an immediate return; help others without using what you give to purchase affection. But compassion must also have wisdom; you must not, out of wanting to keep someone, compromise yourself again and again. Fifth, cultivate mindful observation.

Each day observe each arising of your thoughts;
when you see craving, jealousy, fear and attachment,
do not blame yourself, but know that they are arising.
Only one who can see their own habitual tendencies
has a chance to change them, little by little.
Good man, remember:
to cultivate the mind is not so that some woman will like you,
but so that, even when you meet someone you like,
you can still remain sincere, clear-minded and respectful.

When you know how to love without controlling,
to give without demanding,
to cherish without forcing,
this mind will gradually grow mature.

🔶Plain Rendering

Good man, you ask how to cultivate your own mind first. This question is more important than hurrying to look for the right relationship. Cultivating the mind does not mean turning you into someone else. It means seeing your own habitual tendencies clearly and slowly taming your body, speech and mind. You can begin with five things: First, cultivate non-attachment. When you meet someone you like, do not rush to decide that she is where you belong; and when the other person responds coldly, do not keep guessing and worrying about gain and loss.

You may like someone sincerely; but you cannot, just because you like her, demand that she respond in the same way. Second, cultivate your emotions. When things do not go as you wish, do not rush to reply. When afflictions arise in the mind, first observe your own thoughts, and ask yourself: am I expressing sincerity, or demanding that the other person meet my expectations? Third, cultivate responsibility. Your life, work, finances and commitments should be arranged properly, step by step.

Do not expect that once another person appears, that person will settle all the unease in your life for you. Fourth, cultivate compassion. Care for others without seeking an immediate return; help others without using what you give to buy affection. But compassion must also come with wisdom; you must not, because you want to keep someone, compromise yourself again and again. Fifth, cultivate mindful observation.

Every day, observe each thought as it arises in your mind; when you see craving, jealousy, fear and attachment, there is no need to blame yourself, but you should know that they are arising. Only when you can see your own habitual tendencies will you have a chance to change them slowly. Good man, you should remember: cultivating the mind is not so that some woman will like you, but so that, even when you meet someone you like, you can still remain sincere, rational and respectful.

When you know how to love without controlling,
to give without demanding,
to cherish without forcing,
this mind will gradually grow mature.
