No.28

Is There Less Merit in Reciting Sutras in a Place That Is Not Pure?

In the car, on the road and in daily settings, asking how sutra recitation relates to reverence.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva: because of work, I sometimes recite sutras in the car or outside in places that are not pure. Is the merit and blessing less this way?

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

"A sutra is a mirror of the heart, not of the cleanliness of the house.

If the heart is pure, every word and every verse shines; if the heart is stained, even temples, incense and flowers are empty.

Therefore the merit of reciting sutras lies not in the purity of the place, but in the purity of the heart.

If you recite while walking, wishing protection and peace for all beings; if you recite the Buddha's name in the car, wishing the roads free of harm — all of this is a deed of blessing.

But if you recite with a playful heart or a lazy mind, then even seated in a golden hall, the merit is slight."

🔶Plain Rendering

"A sutra is a mirror of the heart, not of the cleanliness of the house." A sutra is a mirror for the heart; it is not there to reflect whether a house is clean.

"If the heart is pure, every word and verse shines; if the heart is stained, even temples, incense and flowers are empty."

If the heart is pure, every word and every verse you recite gives off light;

if the heart is stained by afflictions, even in a solemn Buddha hall full of incense and flowers, the merit is hollow.

"The merit of reciting lies not in the purity of the place, but in the purity of the heart."

So the merit of reciting sutras does not depend on whether the place is pure, but on whether the heart is pure in that moment.

"Reciting while walking, wishing beings peace; reciting in the car, wishing the roads free of harm — all are deeds of blessing."

If you recite while walking, blessing and protecting all beings in your heart;

if you recite the Buddha's name in the car, praying for safe roads without accidents — these are all acts of blessing.

"But reciting with a playful or lazy mind, even in a golden hall, the merit is slight."

But if you recite in a joking, playful mood or with a careless, lazy mind, then even sitting in a magnificent golden hall, the merit is very slight.
