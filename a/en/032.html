"Seeking Affinity Is Not as Good as Cultivating the Heart; Seeking Gain Without Seeking Clarity Ends in Emptiness"

From asking about the road ahead to looking back at this heart — reminders to turn toward good and lessen attachment.

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

A teaching from the Bodhisattva for all beings.

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

Beings often ask where the road ahead lies,
but seldom ask where this heart is going.
"Many seek affinity,
few cultivate the heart;
many seek to gain,
few seek to understand."
"When you see others succeed, give rise to no envy;
when you see others fail, give rise to no contempt."

"All things in the world
are like spring flowers and autumn leaves —
they gather and they scatter."
"Today's suffering
is not necessarily punishment;
today's gain
is not necessarily reward."
"If you wish to change your fate,
first change your heart;
if you wish to find peace,
first lessen your attachments."

"Each day look at your own faults,
each day cultivate your own heart;
one good thought arising
surpasses a thousand empty words."
