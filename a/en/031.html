No.31

Hurt One Less Person When Angry, Do Not Give Up on Yourself When Low — This Is How Fate Truly Changes

In anger, low points and success, practicing the cultivation of the heart that daily life makes possible.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked: compassionate Bodhisattva, would you give a teaching to all beings in your own way?

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

"The suffering of beings
is mostly not that there is no road;
it is that the heart is in turmoil,
so the road cannot be seen."
"People often seek:
what is the right relationship,
what is heaven's will,
when will luck turn;
yet few ask:
is my heart
clearer today than yesterday?"

"Practice does not leave the world;
compassion does not leave daily life."
"To hurt one less person when angry,
to not abandon oneself when low,
to keep a humble heart when successful —
all of this is practice."
"Do not, because of a moment's setback,
decide that life is hopeless;
do not, because of a moment's good fortune,
forget the flowing of cause and effect."

"Some relationships come to teach us to mature; some suffering comes to wake us up." "To truly change one's fate is not to seek miraculous things; it is to turn one's thoughts day by day and cultivate the heart day by day." "When you see another's suffering, may you have a little more understanding; when you see your own suffering, may you have a little less resentment."

"As the heart grows clearer,
the road ahead gradually opens."

🔶Plain Rendering

"The suffering of beings is mostly not that there is no road; it is that the heart is in turmoil, so the road cannot be seen."

Most of people's suffering is not because there is truly no way out, but because the heart is too confused to see the way.

"People seek the right relationship, heaven's will, when luck will turn; few ask whether their heart is clearer today than yesterday."

People often ask: who is my true match, what is my destiny, when will good luck come? Yet very few ask: is my heart clearer today than it was yesterday?

"Practice does not leave the world; compassion does not leave daily life." Practice is not separate from everyday life, and compassion is not separate from how we treat people each day.

"To hurt one less person when angry, not abandon oneself when low, keep a humble heart when successful — all is practice."

Being able to hurt one less person when you are angry, not giving up on yourself when you are down, and staying humble when things go well — all of these are practice.

"Do not decide life is hopeless because of a moment's setback; do not forget cause and effect because of a moment's good fortune."

Do not think life is hopeless because of a moment of disappointment; do not forget that cause and effect keep turning because of a moment of success.

"Some relationships come to teach us to mature; some suffering comes to wake us up." Some relationships come to help you grow up; some pain comes to wake you up.

"To truly change one's fate is not to seek miracles, but to turn one's thoughts and cultivate the heart day by day."

Truly changing your destiny is not about praying for miracles, but about changing your thoughts and tending your heart every single day.

"When you see another's suffering, a little more understanding; when you see your own, a little less resentment."

When you see others suffering, be willing to understand a little more; when you see yourself suffering, be willing to resent a little less.

"As the heart grows clearer, the road ahead gradually opens."
As the heart becomes clearer, the road ahead gradually opens up.
