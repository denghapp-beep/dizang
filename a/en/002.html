No.02

Wisdom for Outer Circumstances and Inner Peace

When looks, money and the way we speak become burdens, how to see our own worth anew.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

One night in a dream I again received a teaching from Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva.

I said: "Bodhisattva, you once taught me not to belittle myself over outward circumstances. Yet most people care deeply about appearance, finances, how well they speak — and above all about their own inner adjustment. Not everyone is able to calm themselves and release pressure, and not everyone has the chance to meet the Dharma. Would you teach once more, for the world and for me?"

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

"Beings take form to be real and appearance to be true, not knowing that form is illusory and the mind is the root. Look at yourself in a mirror and you see dust, and you grieve; look at yourself with wisdom and you see the Buddha, and you are at peace. Your doubt is the common doubt of all. Know this: outer form waxes and wanes, but the heart within neither arises nor ceases. Wealth and poverty are shadows cast by conditions; only the light within the heart is an indestructible treasure.

Therefore I say: do not take poverty as suffering, do not take your looks as shame. If the heart is ever pure and the mind ever reflects upon itself, then though the form is ordinary, its bearing is noble; though the words are clumsy, the truth stands; though the life is crooked, the virtue is straight. If a person can turn a single thought and see that they lack nothing, a thousand obstacles fall away at once. The practitioner does not seek to change the outer form, only the form of the heart; once the flower of the heart opens, all the heavens are fragrant."

The Bodhisattva asked me to remind the world as well: "A person is honored not for their looks, but valued for their heart." Whoever keeps the light of their own heart amid illusory appearances, though ordinary, is close to the sage.

This teaching is not only for me; it is for everyone with the karmic affinity to read it — all who strive to cultivate the heart in this world, yet are so often shaken by outward things.
