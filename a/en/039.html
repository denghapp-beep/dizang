No.39

Asking Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva How the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas Attain Perfect, Complete Awakening

Through shame, cause and effect, beings and one's own heart — a question on the path to awakening.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva: by what method did you practice to attain perfect, complete awakening?

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

You ask:
by what Dharma did I once attain perfect, complete awakening?"
"Know this:
though the Buddhas appear in the world earlier and later,
and the Bodhisattvas differ in vows and practice,
the truth they realize
is not two."
"It is not a path of mine alone,
nor does each Buddha have a separate path."

"All, by awakening to the nature of mind,
perfecting compassion,
and benefiting beings,
reach the other shore."
"First take conscience and a sense of shame as teacher,
next take cause and effect as teacher,
then take beings as teacher,
and finally take your own heart as teacher."

"Seeing suffering, make vows;
seeing delusion, feel compassion;
seeing your nature, break attachment;
seeing emptiness, do not dwell in emptiness."
"It is not achieved in one sitting,
nor finished in one awakening."
"Practicing through eons as many as dust motes,
every act is the Way;
with awareness in every thought,
every place is a gate."

"If you ask for a shortcut to Buddhahood,
know this:
there is no shortcut."
"If you ask for the nearest road to Buddhahood,
know this:
arousing bodhicitta
is the nearest road."
(End of teaching.)
