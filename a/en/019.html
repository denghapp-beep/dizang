A Teaching for 2026, the Year of the Red Horse and Red Goat

On unease about 2026 and guarding the heart; this inspired record is kept as received.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva: this year is the year of the Red Horse and Red Goat, and many people in Taiwan feel uneasy about Taiwan's future. Every year a national fortune lot is drawn at a major temple in Taiwan. Could the Bodhisattva bestow a lot?

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

"When the world's affairs stir,
the hearts of people stir first;
when people's hearts are in turmoil,
worry arrives before any calamity does.
The Red Horse and Red Goat
are not necessarily disaster;
they mark a year of swift change.
A lot shows the tendency,
not the ending;
the ending lies in what the many do,
not in a single sign.

If the many keep to the ordinary, to goodness, to reason,
ripples do not become waves;
if the many chase alarm, anger and suspicion,
small matters become disasters.
