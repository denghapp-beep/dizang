No.18

Knowing Is Easy, Doing Is Hard: Do Not Be One Who Watches the Boat From the Shore

Principles are a bridge and practice is the crossing — moving from understanding to real action.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva: many Buddhist principles are understood even by people who are not Buddhists, yet when real problems come, we still cannot handle them or get through. Please, compassionate Bodhisattva, give a teaching.

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

"To know without doing is like a lamp that is never lit.
When principles enter the ear but not the heart, wisdom becomes dust.

Beings hear the Dharma in ordinary times like watching a boat from the shore; only when the rapids come do they find they never boarded.

Principle is the bridge; practice is the crossing. If you only recite the bridge's name and will not walk upon it, you will never reach the other shore. Therefore I say:

hearing the Dharma is the cause, practicing the Dharma is the fruit, enduring in the Dharma is the fulfillment.

When difficulties come, if one thought stays unconfused and you hold to these three, there is nothing you cannot get through."

🔶Plain Rendering

Only "understanding" a principle without practicing it is like having a lamp that is never lit.

When a principle stays in the head and never reaches the heart, that understanding is only dust; it cannot light your way.

Listening to sutras and reading the Dharma in ordinary times is like standing on the shore watching a boat; only when the rapids of life rush in do you discover you have not actually boarded.

The teachings of the Dharma are only a bridge; it takes "doing" to truly cross over difficulty. So the Bodhisattva says: hearing the Dharma is the "cause"; putting it into practice with body and life is the "fruit"; being able to endure, hold steady and keep watch in hardship is "accomplishment".

When we are troubled, if we can keep one thought unconfused, remember the Dharma and act according to it, there is no barrier we cannot pass.
