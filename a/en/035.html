No.35

On Same-Sex Love

A question about same-sex love, unfolding through the gathering of conditions, equality and compassion.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked the Bodhisattva for a view on same-sex love.

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

"The hearts of all beings arise from love; love is neither right nor wrong, only stained or pure.

If affection binds affection, one falls into samsara; if affection is transformed into compassion, one is like all Buddhas.

Man and woman, man and man, woman and woman — all are a gathering of conditions, neither fault nor merit. The body is no defect; if the heart is pure, that is the Way.

The practitioner, looking upon beings, should leave discrimination behind; the Buddha's eye sees all beings as equal and without difference."
