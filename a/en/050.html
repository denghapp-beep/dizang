What Is Cause and Effect? What Is Karmic Force?

Through seeds, soil and daily habits, the author's record on cause and effect and karmic force.

Author's record · translated; the Chinese original is authoritative

Open in reading mode →

🔶Inquiry to Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva

I asked Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva:
I humbly ask: what is cause and effect? What is karmic force?

🔶The Bodhisattva's Teaching

"You ask about cause and effect, and also about karmic force. First know this: cause and effect is not reward and punishment; karmic force is not heaven's decree.

A thought arising, a word spoken, a deed done — each can become a cause. A cause meets its conditions, and then becomes an effect. It is like a seed placed in soil: the seed is the cause; water, soil and sunlight are its conditions; when cause and conditions are complete, the fruit is born. So where there is a cause, the effect may not be seen at once; and an effect is not formed by a single cause alone. This is called cause and effect.

What is karma? Beings act with intention: the body does, the mouth speaks, the mind thinks; done again and again, this steeps the heart. A thought today seems very small; repeated day after day, it becomes habit. Habit then makes it easier for similar thoughts and similar deeds to arise. This continuing momentum may be called karmic force.

Karmic force is not a pair of hands punishing you in the dark; it is what beings have done, leaving behind a force that in turn influences later conditions.

One who is often angry grows ever more practiced in anger; even at small things, they flare up easily. One who often deceives grows ever deeper in the habit of deceit; when gain and loss are at stake, they easily deceive again. One who is often kind grows ever more practiced in kindness; seeing beings suffer, they easily reach out a hand. So karma is formed by deeds, and deeds are in turn steeped in karma.

Yet you must understand: to be influenced by karma is not to be locked in by karma. If karma could not be turned, practice would have no meaning; if effects could not be changed, repentance would be useless. Precisely because conditions are not yet fixed, a single thought today can still add new causes. Causes already planted in the past cannot be made never to have happened; but today you can change their conditions, and you can plant new causes as well.

It is like a field that already holds the seeds of weeds: you cannot say the seeds never fell; but you can weed, improve the soil, water it, and plant good seedlings. Over time, the field will look different. This is practice.

So do not, whenever you meet suffering, ask: 'What evil karma did I create?'

A single matter in the world is usually the coming together of many conditions. Illness has bodily causes, accidents have practical causes, and harm done by others also comes from their own choices. Do not, whenever hardship comes, blame it all on karmic retribution from past lives. That is not understanding cause and effect; it is being deluded about cause and effect.

Cause and effect teaches people to take responsibility, not to blame themselves; karmic force teaches people to be careful in what they do, not to resign themselves to fate. What you can hold on to today is this very moment. When an evil thought arises, know it and do not follow it; when harsh words are about to come out, know it and be able to stop; when a good thought arises, let it become action. Each time is small, but over time it runs deep. So do not belittle a single good thought, nor belittle a single evil thought.

Cause and effect lets people know: what is done is not in vain. Karmic force lets people know: what is practiced becomes momentum. Practice lets people know: though momentum is formed, it can still change direction.

If you understand this, you need not frighten yourself with cause and effect, nor use karmic force as an excuse for your choices. Yesterday's karma becomes one of today's conditions; today's heart becomes one of tomorrow's causes.

Therefore I say:
cause and effect is not a verdict; karmic force is not a shackle.
Knowing cause and effect, be careful what you plant;
knowing karmic force, be careful what you practice;
knowing dependent arising, know that things can turn;
knowing they can turn, do not despair."

🔶Plain Rendering

You ask about cause and effect, and also about karmic force.

First you should know: cause and effect is not reward and punishment, and karmic force is not destiny decreed by heaven.

A thought that arises, a word that is spoken, a deed that is done — each can become a "cause". A cause meets its "conditions", and only then does it form an "effect".

It is like a seed buried in the soil: the seed is the cause; soil, water and sunlight are the conditions; only when cause and conditions are complete does fruit appear.

So having a cause does not mean you will see the result right away; and a result is not produced by just one single cause. This is cause and effect.

What is karma?

Beings act intentionally — the body acts, the mouth speaks, the mind thinks — and doing this over and over shapes their own hearts.

One thought today looks very small; but repeated every day, it becomes a habit. Habits then make it easier for similar thoughts and similar behavior to arise. This continuing force is called karmic force.

Karmic force is not a pair of hands secretly punishing you; it is the force left behind by what beings themselves have done, which in turn affects the conditions that follow.

When a person is often angry, their anger becomes more and more practiced; even small things easily set them off. When a person often lies, the habit of lying grows deeper; when gain or loss is at stake, they easily lie again. When a person is often kind, their kindness becomes more and more practiced; when they see others suffering, they easily reach out to help.

So karma is formed by actions, and actions in turn are shaped by karma. However, you must understand: being influenced by karma does not mean being locked in by karma.

If karma could not change at all, practice would have no meaning; if results could not change at all, repentance would be useless.

It is precisely because conditions are not yet fixed that a single thought today can still add a new cause.

Causes already planted in the past cannot be made to have never happened; but today you can change their conditions, and you can also plant new causes.

For example, if a field already has weed seeds in it, you cannot say the seeds never fell; but you can pull the weeds, improve the soil, water it, and plant good seedlings. Over time, the field will look different. This is practice.

So do not, whenever you meet hardship, ask: "What bad karma did I create?" Things in the world usually come from many conditions together. Illness has bodily causes, accidents have practical causes, and when others hurt you, that also comes from their own choices. You must not blame every hardship on retribution from past lives. That is not understanding cause and effect, but being confused about it.

Cause and effect teaches people to take responsibility, not to blame themselves; karmic force teaches people to be careful in what they do, not to accept fate passively.

What you can hold on to today is this moment. When a bad thought arises, notice it and do not follow it; when harsh words are about to come out, notice it and be able to stop; when a good thought arises, let it become action. Each time is small, but over time it goes deep.

So do not underestimate one good thought, and do not underestimate one bad thought.

Cause and effect lets people know that what we do is never in vain. Karmic force lets people know that what we repeatedly practice becomes a force. Practice lets people know that even though a force has formed, it can still change direction.

If you understand this, you will not need to frighten yourself with cause and effect, and you must not use karmic force as an excuse for your own choices.

Yesterday's karma becomes one of today's conditions; today's heart becomes one of tomorrow's causes. So I say:

cause and effect is not a verdict, and karmic force is not a shackle. Knowing cause and effect, be careful what you plant; knowing karmic force, be careful what you repeat; knowing dependent arising, know that things can change; knowing they can change, do not despair.
